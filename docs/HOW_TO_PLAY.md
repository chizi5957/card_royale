# How to Play — Card Royale (The Bidding War)

## The Goal

Win more prize points than your opponent over 13 rounds of bidding.

---

## Setup

- **You** get 13 cards — the full Spades suit (♠ A through K) if you're Player 1, or Clubs (♣) if you're Player 2
- **Your opponent** gets the other suit
- A **prize deck** of 26 shuffled red cards (Hearts ♥ and Diamonds ♦) sits face-up in the middle — one card revealed per round

---

## Each Round

1. **Look at your hand** — choose a card you want to play
2. **Click the card**, then press **Play**
3. **Both cards are revealed at the same time**
4. The player who played the **higher card** wins the prize card
5. Move to the next round — the played cards are gone forever

> Card values: A=1, 2=2, 3=3 ... 10=10, J=11, Q=12, K=13

---

## Ties

If both players play the same value card, nobody wins the prize. Instead:
- The prize card is **carried over** to the next round
- Next round there are now **2 (or more) prize cards** at stake
- This can stack across multiple tie rounds — big jackpots possible!

---

## Scoring

After all 13 rounds, count up the **point values** of all prize cards you've won. Highest total wins.

---

## Multiplayer (Online)

1. Player 1 clicks **Create Game** — gets a 6-letter code
2. Player 2 clicks **Join Game** — enters the code
3. Once Player 2 joins, the game starts automatically
4. Both players play from their own browser — cards are hidden until both have played

---

## vs Bot

Click **Play vs Bot** from the lobby. The bot picks cards randomly. No internet or database needed for bot mode.

---

## Tips

- High cards are powerful — but once played, they're gone
- A low card thrown on a cheap prize saves your big cards for later
- Watch which cards your opponent has already played (shown in the "Last Round" section)
- When prizes carry over, the stakes are high — gamble or play it safe?
